I am an **Independent AI & Machine Learning Researcher** dedicated to exploring the theoretical foundations of deep learning, neural network dynamics, and information theory. My research focuses on developing robust mathematical frameworks to understand how information flows, scales, and transforms within complex network architectures.

### Research Interests
* **Deep Learning Theory:** Optimization landscapes, generalization properties, and neural network dynamics.
* **Information Dynamics:** The intersection of information theory and computational mechanics applied to machine learning models.
* **Network Dynamics:** Analyzing complex dependencies and algorithmic behavior in neuro-inspired structures.

By maintaining an independent research path, I focus on bridging the gap between theoretical computer science and practical AI scalability. I am always open to academic collaborations, research discussions, and professional consulting opportunities in the field of advanced Machine Learning.
